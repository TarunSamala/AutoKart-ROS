E-STOP RECEIVER PINOUT
======================

ESP32-S3 GPIO14  -> RA-02 SCK
ESP32-S3 GPIO13  -> RA-02 MOSI
ESP32-S3 GPIO15  -> RA-02 MISO
ESP32-S3 GPIO16  -> RA-02 NSS/CS
ESP32-S3 GPIO17  -> RA-02 RESET
ESP32-S3 GPIO18  -> RA-02 DIO0
ESP32-S3 3.3 V   -> RA-02 VCC
ESP32-S3 GND     -> RA-02 GND

Frequency is 433 MHz. Connect a correct 433 MHz antenna before transmitting or
receiving. Verify that these GPIOs are exposed on the exact ESP32-S3 board.
