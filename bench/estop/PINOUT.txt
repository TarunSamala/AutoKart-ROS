WIRELESS E-STOP PINOUT SUMMARY
==============================

ESP32-S3 receiver: GPIO14 SCK, GPIO13 MOSI, GPIO15 MISO, GPIO16 NSS/CS,
GPIO17 RESET, GPIO18 DIO0.

Raspberry Pi Pico transmitter: GPIO18 SCK, GPIO19 MOSI, GPIO16 MISO,
GPIO17 NSS/CS, GPIO20 RESET, GPIO21 DIO0, GPIO22 E-stop input.

Use 3.3 V logic and the RA-02 module's required stable 3.3 V supply. Do not
power the RA-02 from 5 V. Follow the device-specific PINOUT.txt files.
