LORA DIAGNOSTIC PINOUT
======================

Receiver test (ESP32-S3):
  GPIO14 SCK, GPIO13 MOSI, GPIO15 MISO, GPIO16 NSS/CS,
  GPIO17 RESET, GPIO18 DIO0.

Transmitter test (Raspberry Pi Pico):
  GPIO18 SCK, GPIO19 MOSI, GPIO16 MISO, GPIO17 NSS/CS,
  GPIO20 RESET, GPIO21 DIO0.

Both RA-02 modules require 3.3 V power/logic, common ground, and an appropriate
433 MHz antenna.
