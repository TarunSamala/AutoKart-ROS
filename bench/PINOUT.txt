AUTOKART BENCH MASTER PINOUT
============================

Integrated ESP32-S3 assignment
------------------------------

GPIO1   BLD-750 EN transistor driver
GPIO2   BLD-750 BRK transistor driver
GPIO4   Steering TB6600 PUL+
GPIO5   Steering TB6600 DIR+
GPIO6   Steering TB6600 ENA+
GPIO7   BLD-750 F/R transistor driver
GPIO8   GP8630N SDA
GPIO9   GP8630N SCL
GPIO10  Brake TB6600 PUL+
GPIO11  Brake TB6600 DIR+
GPIO12  Brake TB6600 ENA+
GPIO13  RA-02 MOSI
GPIO14  RA-02 SCK
GPIO15  RA-02 MISO
GPIO16  RA-02 NSS/CS
GPIO17  RA-02 RESET
GPIO18  RA-02 DIO0

All TB6600 negative signal terminals connect to ESP32 GND for the documented
single-ended wiring. GP8630N GND, BLD-750 COM, logic-interface ground, and the
appropriate controller ground must share the intended reference. Confirm
isolation and voltage compatibility before wiring.

Raspberry Pi Pico E-stop transmitter
------------------------------------

GPIO16  RA-02 MISO
GPIO17  RA-02 NSS/CS
GPIO18  RA-02 SCK
GPIO19  RA-02 MOSI
GPIO20  RA-02 RESET
GPIO21  RA-02 DIO0
GPIO22  E-stop switch input; LOW released, HIGH pressed

See each subsystem PINOUT.txt before making connections.
