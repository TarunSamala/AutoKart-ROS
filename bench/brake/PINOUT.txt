MECHANICAL BRAKE PINOUT
=======================

ESP32-S3 GPIO10  -> Brake TB6600 PUL+
ESP32-S3 GPIO11  -> Brake TB6600 DIR+
ESP32-S3 GPIO12  -> Brake TB6600 ENA+
ESP32-S3 GND     -> Brake TB6600 PUL-, DIR-, and ENA-

Documented TB6600 switch assumption: S1 OFF, S2 OFF, S3 ON, interpreted by the
current setup as 3200 pulses/revolution. TB6600 clone tables vary; verify the
label on the actual driver before running the test.
