RECOVERY INDEX PINOUT NOTICE
============================

The recovery index describes multiple incompatible historical pin maps.

Current integrated map:
  BLD-750 control GPIO1/2/7
  Steering GPIO4/5/6
  GP8630N GPIO8/9
  Brake GPIO10/11/12
  RA-02 receiver GPIO13/14/15/16/17/18

Historical BLD-750 record:
  One unavailable revision is described as GPIO13/14/15 for EN/BRK/F-R.
  Do not combine that map with the LoRa receiver because those pins overlap.
