E-STOP TRANSMITTER PINOUT
=========================

Pico GPIO18  -> RA-02 SCK
Pico GPIO19  -> RA-02 MOSI
Pico GPIO16  -> RA-02 MISO
Pico GPIO17  -> RA-02 NSS/CS
Pico GPIO20  -> RA-02 RESET
Pico GPIO21  -> RA-02 DIO0
Pico GPIO22  -> E-stop switch signal
Pico 3.3 V   -> RA-02 VCC and switch high side as designed
Pico GND     -> RA-02 GND

GPIO22 uses an internal pull-down: released must read LOW and pressed HIGH.
Use a 433 MHz antenna and never apply 5 V to RA-02 logic or power pins.
