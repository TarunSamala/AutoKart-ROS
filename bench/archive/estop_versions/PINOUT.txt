ARCHIVED E-STOP PINOUT
======================

The original receiver revisions use ESP32-S3 GPIO14 SCK, GPIO13 MOSI, GPIO15
MISO, GPIO16 NSS/CS, GPIO17 RESET, and GPIO18 DIO0.

The original transmitter revisions use Pico GPIO18 SCK, GPIO19 MOSI, GPIO16
MISO, GPIO17 NSS/CS, GPIO20 RESET, GPIO21 DIO0, and GPIO22 for the switch.

Refer to the active E-stop PINOUT.txt files before wiring.
