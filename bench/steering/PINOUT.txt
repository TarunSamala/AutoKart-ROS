STEERING PINOUT
===============

ESP32-S3 GPIO4  -> Steering TB6600 PUL+
ESP32-S3 GPIO5  -> Steering TB6600 DIR+
ESP32-S3 GPIO6  -> Steering TB6600 ENA+
ESP32-S3 GND    -> Steering TB6600 PUL-, DIR-, and ENA-

The code assumes active-low enable, 3200 pulses/motor revolution, and a 17T to
51T gear set. Verify the exact TB6600 switch table and physical direction.
